Privacy notice

Render the Molecule · last updated 14 November 2026

Before rendering or saving a preview image, we ask for your name, email address, institution and country. Your information will be used to understand academic adoption and improve the application. It will not be shared or used for marketing without your separate consent.

What is recorded
Your name, email address, institution and country, together with what you render or save as a preview image: the PDB ID, the options and view you chose, the date and time, and whether it worked. A preview image itself is made in your browser and is not sent to us. Your browser also remembers your details on your own device so you don't have to type them again. We use no cookies, adverts or trackers.
Who keeps it
Vishal Bhoir, the developer of Render the Molecule, in a private Google Sheet.
Services involved
The web app runs on Modal, the notebook on Google Colab, and the records are stored with Google. These services may process data outside your country.
How long
Only as long as needed for the purposes above.
Legal basis
Our legitimate interest in understanding how this free research tool is used.
Your choices
You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to its use, at any time by contacting Vishal Bhoir. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
